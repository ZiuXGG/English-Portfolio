Who am I?

Engineering & Software TdR: Deepfake Audio ML K1 Kickboxing & Running Sci-Fi & Psychological Thrillers

Hi there, my name is Youssef, and in this portfolio I’d like to give you a look into who I am, what drives me and everything I'd learned this year.

Academically, I have always been drawn to the intersection of engineering, software development, and the exact sciences, driven by a curiosity to break down complex systems and understand how they truly work. For me, programming is more than just writing code; it is a creative tool for solving real-world problems through logic—an approach I applied in my research project (TdR) on machine learning and audio signal processing, where I built models to detect deepfake audio.

That same appreciation for strategy and intricate design carries into my downtime, whether I am sharpening my split-second decision-making and tactical coordination through competitive gaming or exploring science fiction and psychological thrillers rich in complex themes and narrative structures.

Beyond technology, physical training is a fundamental pillar of my daily routine: K1 kickboxing and running demand deep commitment and resilience, teaching me that true progress comes from consistency and adaptability—discipline-building habits that directly translate into my academic life and keep me focused when tackling difficult challenges.
